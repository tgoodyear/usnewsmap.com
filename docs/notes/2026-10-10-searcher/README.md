# Searcher experiments, 9 and 10 October 2026 (#251)

**Outcome:** #251 closed on 10 October 2026 and the dev environment was torn down (#275, closed 15 October 2026). Search now runs from our Quickwit fork ([ADR-0013](../../design/adr/0013-quickwit-from-our-fork.md)) and is served on-prem ([ADR-0027](../../design/adr/0027-onprem-serving-behind-cloudflare.md)).

Reports generated by `scripts/qwcluster-report.py` (now [`scripts/azure/qwcluster-report.py`](../../../scripts/azure/qwcluster-report.py)) from the dev environment's Log Analytics before dev was torn down (#275). The findings are summarized on #251. A third report, per-split resource stats and CPU profiles re-logged from stored runs, was never committed here.

- [`version-comparison.md`](version-comparison.md): Quickwit 0.9.1 against a pre-0.9 build on the same splits (0.9.1 about 3x faster).
- [`blob-nfs-patched.md`](blob-nfs-patched.md): Blob, NFS and the patched Quickwit (pooled Azure client, `tgoodyear/quickwit` tag `usnm-v0.9.1-pool1`) on the 1% index at the sidecar's shape. The patched build cut cold search time fourfold and raised throughput at 10 at once from 1.5 to about 10 searches/s.

The `loc-cache-*` and `loc-copy-*` rows in these reports are invalid: those revisions never started (see the correction on #251).
