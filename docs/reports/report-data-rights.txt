Report data: source attribution and redistribution
==================================================

This notice accompanies the report-data archives. The cumulative report-data
release has a file-by-file SHA-256 inventory, report-data-manifest.json in
this directory, which covers the October archive. A report with its own set
in ops/data-assets.json (report-YYYY-MM-topic) is released separately, with
its member list and SHA256SUMS as release assets. The GPU studies' data is in
data-report-2026-11-gpu-studies-20261106-0183479. The copy of this notice in
that release has the earlier wording; its source terms are the same. The
October release and the Study 8 supplement are unchanged.

There is no single licence for the archive. The source terms below apply to
the material they cover, not automatically to every file or to every output
of a model trained on a source. Several files mix sources. Identify rows by
their crop or page IDs, the sample lists, and the relevant report and protocol
before applying a source's terms. OCR readings can contain newspaper text;
they are not just scores. Each redistribution must retain the applicable
attribution and meet the source's conditions. The maintainer considers this
research-data release noncommercial; other users must assess their own use.

Where to find the sources
-------------------------

Paths below are relative to the repository root. They describe the archives'
contents, not all possible future files under those directories.

* docs/data/: storage inventory and LOC metadata, not benchmark reference
  datasets.
* docs/reports/2026-10-boinc-quality-pilot/data/calibration/: Overproof,
  Reichsanzeiger and BLN600 score files; the qwen sample35 JSONL files contain
  readings on Overproof / LOC crops. BLN600 files contain IDs and metrics.
* docs/reports/2026-10-boinc-quality-pilot/data/campaigns/: LOC-derived
  samples, with OCR and VLM text in crop tables, including Parquet copies.
* docs/reports/2026-10-iris-model-reuse/: per-crop model readings on mixed
  Overproof and Reichsanzeiger inputs, plus timings and telemetry. Crop IDs
  distinguish the sources.
* docs/reports/2026-10-jp2-vs-jpeg-ocr/ and 2026-10-ocr-engine-survey/:
  LOC-page samples, scores and diagnostics. The full source OCR and images
  stayed on the study nodes.
* docs/reports/2026-10-language-analysis/: LOC and American Stories-derived
  metadata and aggregates. data/issue-329/flags.jsonl.gz contains words and
  short contexts from OCR; labels.json also contains words.
* docs/reports/2026-10-surya-volunteer/: scores on Overproof and
  Reichsanzeiger benchmark subsets, not the reference texts.
* docs/reports/2026-11-gpu-studies/data/: ja/ contains reader outputs on LOC
  Japanese newspaper crops. s1/ uses Reichsanzeiger, NZZ and LOC pilot pages;
  s2/ uses Reichsanzeiger, Overproof, NZZ and NewsEye Finnish validation;
  s3/ and s6/ use the mixed benchmark and LOC page samples; s4/ uses LOC
  pilot pages; s5/ includes Reichsanzeiger, Overproof, NZZ, NewsEye and
  GT4HistOCR readings and BLN600 metrics; s7/ uses Austrian, NewsEye French,
  Finnish and Swedish, and LOC crops; s9/ uses the mixed benchmark, LOC pages
  and Japanese samples. The files include reader outputs as well as scores.
  Crop IDs and the protocol's source-disjoint sample descriptions distinguish
  the sources.

The archive does not include every upstream image or reference needed to rerun
the studies. The GPU studies' frozen ID lists are in
data-report-2026-11-gpu-studies-20261106-0183479; consult the reports and
benchmark source manifests to obtain the inputs separately under their own
terms. Retained outputs and logs also have the reproducibility limitations
recorded in the GPU study report.
