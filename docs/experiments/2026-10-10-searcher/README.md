# Searcher experiments, 9 and 10 October 2026 (#251)

Reports generated by `scripts/qwcluster-report.py` from the dev environment's Log Analytics before dev was torn down (#275). The findings are summarized on #251.

- `version-comparison.md`: Quickwit 0.9.1 against a pre-0.9 build on the same splits (0.9.1 about 3x faster).
- `probe-dump.md`: per-split resource stats and CPU profiles re-logged from stored runs.
- `blob-nfs-patched.md`: Blob, NFS and the patched Quickwit (pooled Azure client, `tgoodyear/quickwit` tag `usnm-v0.9.1-pool1`) on the 1% index at the sidecar's shape. The patched build cut cold search time 4 times and raised throughput at 10 at once from 1.5 to about 10 searches/s.

The `loc-cache-*` and `loc-copy-*` rows in these reports are invalid: those revisions never started (see the correction on #251).
