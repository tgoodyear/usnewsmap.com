THE EVENING GAZETTE

The council met on Monday to

discuss the new bridge over the
river. Members agreed that the

construc-

tion should begin in the spring

and finish before the winter.

Café Miiller opens on Fifth Street — fine coffee, cakes and pies.

Prices for wheat & corn rose
again this week at the market,
while cotton fell by two cents.

Farmers said the "late frost"
hurt the early crop badly.
