# Tell lists

Used by the mechanical sweep in `check.py`. One hit is weak evidence. Several in one passage are the tell. Leave a word alone inside a quotation, a product name, code, a historical source, or a passage that discusses the word.

## Stock vocabulary

delve, bolster, illuminate, elucidate, transcend, intertwine, underscore, tapestry, synergy, beacon, interplay, intricate, intricacies, myriad, plethora, vibrant, meticulous, multifaceted, pivotal, paramount, testament, realm, landscape (abstract), garner, showcase, foster, leverage, utilize, facilitate, nestled, enduring, commendable, unparalleled, ever-evolving, cutting-edge, game-changing, groundbreaking, holistic, nuanced (as praise)

Phrases: shed light on, grapple with, navigate the complexities, paving the way, rich tapestry, rich history, stands as a testament, plays a vital role, a pivotal moment, deep dive, at its core, in today's world, in an ever-changing landscape, look no further

## Marketing register (fine in an ad, a tell anywhere else)

empower, unlock, elevate, seamless, seamlessly, streamline, revolutionize, transform (as hype), supercharge, harness the power of, next-level, best-in-class, world-class, explore (as a site verb), discover, journey (for a product or process), one-stop, effortless, marketplace, exchange

## Filler transitions (at sentence start)

Additionally, Furthermore, Moreover, Importantly, Crucially, Critically, Notably, Ultimately, In conclusion, Lastly, That said, Needless to say, In other words, At the end of the day, As we can see

## Staging

Here's the thing, Here's what, Here's why, Let me be clear, Let me explain, Let's break it down, Let's dive in, What nobody tells you, What most people miss, The real story is, The truth is, The best part, The bottom line, Key takeaway, That's the real win, Let that sink in, Thoughts?, What do you think?

Constructions: "not just X but Y", "It's not X, it's Y", "isn't about X. It's about Y", "Y rather than X" (as a reveal), "serves as", "stands as", "boasts a", "a testament to"

## Filler hedges

arguably, it's worth noting, it is worth noting, it's important to note, it is important to note, it could potentially, may potentially, to some extent, in many ways

## Op-ed attribution verbs

posits, contends, asserts, maintains that. Use says, writes, found, reports.
